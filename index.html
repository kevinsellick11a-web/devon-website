The Setting

The World

A world held together by covenants, threads, and the things people choose not to see.

Aevrath

A kingdom shaped by old agreements between gods and mortals. The covenant that governs it was signed without the consent of everyone it bound.

The Threads

Invisible connections between people, places, and things. Gold for mortal threads. Only a handful of people have ever been able to see them.

The Author

Devon Maren

Devon Maren writes epic fantasy about the cost of truth, the weight of choice, and the people who keep going anyway.

The Shattered Covenant is his debut trilogy.
